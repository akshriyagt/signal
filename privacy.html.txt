<h1>Privacy Policy — Signal Call Analyzer</h1>
<p class="updated">Last updated: August 29, 2026</p>

<p>Signal ("the App") is a call recording analysis tool. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.</p>

<h2>1. What We Collect</h2>
<p>When you use the App, you choose audio recordings (call recordings or other audio files) from your device and upload them for analysis. We collect:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The audio file(s) you choose to upload</li>
  <li>The transcribed text generated from those files</li>
  <li>Basic classification labels (e.g. spam, important, normal) generated by the App</li>
</ul>
<p>The App does not automatically access your call log, contacts, or any files you do not explicitly select.</p>

<h2>2. How We Use Your Data</h2>
<p>Uploaded audio is sent securely to our processing server, where it is transcribed using speech-to-text technology and analyzed to classify the call. The resulting transcript and classification are shown back to you in the App.</p>

<h2>3. Data Storage</h2>
<p>Audio files and transcripts are processed on our server to generate your results. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.</p>

<h2>4. Third-Party Services</h2>
<p>The App uses a speech-to-text engine to generate transcriptions. No data is shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.</p>

<h2>5. Your Choices</h2>
<p>You control which files are uploaded to the App — nothing is uploaded without your action. You may stop using the App at any time.</p>

<h2>6. Children's Privacy</h2>
<p>The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.</p>

<h2>7. Changes to This Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.</p>
